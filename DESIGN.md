# Case-study navigation glass treatment

## Reference

- Source: supplied screenshot, inspected 2026-11-13.

## Observed pattern

- The surrounding surface is nearly white and intentionally recedes.
- Recessed and selected controls are communicated with subtle light-gray fill changes, a hairline edge, a white inset highlight, and a very soft shadow.
- Contrast is reserved for the content, not for the control container.

## Portfolio adaptation

The case-study navigation has no colored rail or container. Inactive links sit directly on the page surface in the portfolio's muted ink; the selected link becomes a pale neutral tile with a hairline edge and restrained inset/outset depth. Hover is a slight white wash only.

This applies the reference's light, low-emphasis control hierarchy to a text navigation context rather than copying its credit-stepper layout.
